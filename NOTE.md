# Pick a business process you know well. What would you automate first, and how would you know it worked?

**The process: choosing which accounts an outbound rep researches next.**
A rep starts the week with a long list and no reason to pick one company over another, so research
becomes guesswork or hours of reading career pages. For a sales-compensation vendor it is harder, because
the buyer is rare: often one person per company runs commissions.

**What I would automate first: the evidence-gathering, not the outreach.**
CompSignal reads target companies' public job boards each week and flags explicit signs that a commission
operation is in motion: an open Commissions Manager role, a posting that names one compensation platform, or
commission work tied to spreadsheets. Each brief links its evidence so the rep can disagree. Scoring is rule-based,
with no AI judgement, so any number can be challenged.

Testing it taught me something. Of 150 reachable companies, 14 had a strict signal. In two labelled samples
(40 and 24 flags), 88% and 92% were extracted correctly, but only 36% and 14% of platform mentions pointed to a
platform the company actually runs. Most were "experience with A, B or C" lists, so I split "specific" from "listed".

**How I would know it worked**
1. At least 80% of flagged evidence confirmed on a fresh labelled sample each month.
2. Reps call at least half the weekly briefs worth a first touch.
3. Research time per account falls against a few accounts done by hand.
4. Reply and meeting rates beat a comparable unflagged group.

If accuracy drops below the bar, or reps ignore the briefs for a month, I would narrow the signals or retire it.
